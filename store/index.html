Frequently asked questions

Cosmetics, payments and refunds.

What are Axquen Client cosmetics?

Optional, purely visual items, cloaks, hats, wings, and particle auras, that render on your in-game character when you play Minecraft through the Axquen Client launcher. They never modify blocks, items, stats, or gameplay, and cannot grant any competitive advantage.

Who can see my cosmetics?

Other players see your cosmetics only if they also have the Axquen Client mod installed. On vanilla servers, anti-cheat servers, or servers where the operator has disabled the cosmetic channel, your cosmetics will simply not render to others. Your own first-person view is unaffected.

How do I buy and equip a cosmetic?

Click Sign In to Purchase and authenticate with your Microsoft account (the same Xbox Live OAuth flow used by the official Minecraft launcher). Then click any cosmetic to check out securely through Tebex.

After purchase the cosmetic appears in your launcher's Cosmetics tab within seconds. Equip it from there, launch any version of Minecraft, and it will render the next time you join a world or server.

What payment methods are accepted?

Checkout is hosted by Tebex, a registered Merchant of Record. You can pay with Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Google Pay, or PaysafeCard. VAT is calculated automatically based on your billing region. We never see or store your full card number, only the order reference and your account email.

Can I earn cosmetics without paying?

Yes. Axquen Points are the in-launcher currency, earned automatically while you play (100 per hour, capped at 200 per day and 1,000 per week). They never expire. Most paid cosmetics also have a Axquen Points price, and several cosmetics are free to claim once you sign in.

What do the rarity tags mean?

Cosmetics are tagged Common, Rare, Epic, or Legendary. The rarity reflects the visual complexity and effort that went into the model and texture. It does not affect rendering quality or in-game behaviour.

Are purchases refundable?

Cosmetic purchases are non-refundable. Because a cosmetic is digital and is delivered to your Axquen Client account the moment payment clears, we cannot revert the entitlement once granted. By completing checkout you expressly consent to immediate delivery and waive the EU 14-day distance-selling withdrawal right that would otherwise apply.

See the full refund language and your statutory rights in our Terms of Service.

Which regions are supported?

The store is available in every region where Tebex operates, including the EU, UK, United States, Canada, Australia, and most other major markets. Our privacy policy documents the full list of data shared with the payment processor.

Do I own my cosmetics permanently?

Yes. You own each cosmetic permanently against your Axquen Client account, no rental, no expiry, no subscription required to wear what you have already bought.
