Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Leaf Client ("Leaf Client," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website leafclient.com and distributes a Minecraft Fabric client consisting of a launcher application and a mod. We also operate an online store for optional cosmetic items such as capes, wings, and particle auras. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, launcher, mod, or store, how we use and share that information, and the rights you have over your personal data.

Leaf Client is developed and published by ATLAS STUDIOS LLC, an Illinois limited liability company, which is the data controller for the purposes of this policy. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Mojang Studios, Microsoft, or any other third party mentioned in this policy. By using leafclient.com or any Leaf Client software, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the service.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what we need to run the service. The categories below describe everything we receive, store, or process.

2.1 Account information

When you create a Leaf Client account you provide a username, an email address, and either a password (for offline Leaf Client accounts, used when you don't sign in with Microsoft) or an OAuth identifier from Microsoft (for authenticated Minecraft accounts). For accounts you create with a password, a valid email address is required and must be verified (by entering a short code we email you) before you can sign in; accounts created by signing in with Microsoft are authenticated through Microsoft and do not require a separate Leaf Client email. We use your email to confirm ownership of the account, to let you reset a forgotten password and recover access, and to send account-related notices. Passwords are never stored in plaintext; we store a one-way hash produced with a modern key-derivation function. For Microsoft OAuth, we store the opaque user identifier and short-lived refresh tokens necessary to keep your session active.

2.2 Cosmetics and entitlement data

If you purchase or unlock cosmetic items we record the entitlement against your account: which items you own, when they were granted, and the payment reference supplied by our payment processor. We do not store full card numbers, bank details, or PayPal credentials on our servers.

2.3 Usage and diagnostic data

The launcher and mod connect to api.leafclient.com to sign you in, verify entitlements, fetch updates, and download cosmetic assets. What each kind of request tells us is set out below.

  • Request logs. Every request to our API passes through Cloudflare and our web server, which record the time, the endpoint, the method and response status, the user-agent, the client version, and the IP address of the requesting host. Cloudflare also passes our server the two-letter country code it derives from that IP address. We use these logs for rate limiting, abuse prevention, and debugging, and for nothing else. Section 7 gives the retention.
  • Country counts. When your client sends a presence heartbeat we read that two-letter country code, add you to a daily count for that country, and discard the code. We never store a country against your account, and the stored figure is one number per country per day with no identifier in it. Section 7 gives the retention.
  • Presence heartbeats. While the launcher is open, and while you are in a game with the mod loaded, your client sends a periodic heartbeat so that online counts and your friends list are accurate. The launcher heartbeat carries only your Minecraft username and the fact that it came from the launcher. The mod heartbeat also carries the address of the Minecraft server you are playing on, along with your Minecraft username, the fact that it came from in game, and whether you have been active at the keyboard recently. We keep one current row per client, including that server address, and delete it after seven days without a heartbeat. The "hide server" setting stops your server being shown to other people, and the "invisible" setting hides your presence entirely.
  • Playtime. While you are in a game the launcher periodically sends the number of minutes played so that coins can be awarded. It sends a number of minutes and nothing else: no server, no game version, no world.
  • Server Discovery joins. If you join a Minecraft server that is published in Leaf Client's Server Discovery, we record that your account joined that listing on that calendar day, together with your account type, the weight that join contributed to the listing's ranking, and a keyed, day-scoped hash of your IP address that stops one person inflating a listing from many accounts. These rows are deleted after thirty days. We do not record joins to servers that are not published in Discovery.
  • Launcher logs you upload. If you send us your launcher log, we store the log text, your launcher version, and your platform against your account, and keep the most recent uploads for a limited period. A launcher log contains file paths, which on most systems include the name of your operating-system user account.
  • Server status and avatars. When the launcher shows you the status of a Minecraft server in your server list it asks a third-party service about that address. When it draws a player avatar or skin preview it asks one of several third-party render services. Those requests tell that third party the server address or Minecraft username in question, and your IP address, because your computer makes the request directly. Section 5 names them.

None of this includes your Minecraft world data, your chat, your screenshots, or your keystrokes.

2.4 Device and browser information

When you visit leafclient.com we automatically receive information typical of any web request: IP address, user-agent string, referring page, and the time of access. This information is handled by our hosting provider and, where applicable, by third-party services described in Section 5.

2.5 Cookies and local storage

We use cookies and browser local storage to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and, in the future, measure aggregate traffic. Section 4 describes this in detail.

2.6 Communications

If you email us, open an issue on GitHub, or message us through Discord, we receive whatever you send. We retain those messages for as long as is needed to respond and to keep a support record.

2.7 Software data handling

All Leaf Client software (installer, launcher, updater, and client mod) communicates with our backend over HTTPS. The installer and updater collect no personal data. The launcher contacts our API for authentication, cosmetic entitlements, and updates. The client mod reads your cosmetic entitlements and sends periodic heartbeats for online counts. None of our software transmits Minecraft world data, chat logs, screenshots, or keystrokes to our servers. Our software itself contains no advertising.

2.8 Abuse-prevention identifiers

To prevent fraud, account-creation abuse, and trial-stacking against features such as the Leaf+ free trial and the referral program, the launcher derives a stable, one-way hashed device identifier and sends it to api.leafclient.com alongside specific eligibility checks. We refer to this value as a "device hash."

  • What it is. The launcher reads two stable device values from your operating system (the Windows installation GUID, or its equivalent on Linux/macOS, and the MAC address of the first network adapter), concatenates them with a fixed Leaf Client salt, and produces a SHA-256 hash. Only the hash is transmitted; the original values never leave your device.
  • What it is not. The device hash is not a telemetry beacon or tracking cookie, and it is not a broad profile of your hardware. It is a per-device identifier built only from the two values described above and sent only as a one-way SHA-256 hash. We never receive or store your raw MAC address, GUID, IP, hostname, CPU, GPU, motherboard serial, or disk serial, and the hash cannot be reversed to recover them.
  • When it is sent. At account creation, and at the moments where abuse prevention is required, such as claiming a free trial or redeeming a referral code. It is not sent on every API call and not sent during ordinary gameplay.
  • How it is used. The server checks whether the same hash has already redeemed a one-shot benefit (for example, a 7-day Leaf+ trial) and rejects the second request. It also limits how many accounts can be created from the same physical device, and helps rate-limit abusive sign-up patterns that would otherwise bypass IP-based limits (for example, through a VPN).
  • Retention. Device hashes associated with consumed one-shot benefits are kept for as long as that benefit needs to remain non-redeemable on that device (typically the lifetime of the account). Hashes captured purely for rate-limiting are purged in line with our general API log retention, which Section 7 states.
  • Your choices. Creating an account requires the device hash so we can enforce the limits above, so it is sent during sign-up. You can still decline optional one-shot benefits like the free trial or a referral code, and ordinary gameplay on an existing account never sends it.

2.9 Leaf+ Cloud Sync

Cloud Sync is a Leaf+ feature that keeps your setup the same on every computer you sign in on. It runs only while you have an active Leaf+ subscription, and it is what the "cloud sync" perk on the Leaf+ page refers to. When it runs, the launcher uploads to us:

  • Your Leaf Client launcher settings, including your installed mod list, your interface preferences, your equipped cosmetics, and your saved account usernames and UUIDs. Your passwords and every authentication token are removed before upload and never leave your computer.
  • Your Leaf Client mod settings.
  • Your Minecraft multiplayer server list, read from servers.dat in your Minecraft folder and uploaded as it is. This is the list of servers you have saved in Minecraft, including any private ones.
  • The resource-pack lines from your Minecraft options.txt.

It does not upload your worlds, your saves, your screenshots, your Minecraft logs, or any other file. It uploads once when the launcher starts and again whenever your settings change. If you would rather not have your server list stored with us, cancel Leaf+ or ask us at [email protected] to delete your synced data, and we will remove it.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Create and maintain your account, authenticate you, and keep your session active across devices.
  • Grant and verify cosmetic entitlements, including delivering cloaks, wings, and particle auras to your in-game character.
  • Process payments through our payment processors and respond to chargebacks or refund requests.
  • Provide, update, and secure the launcher, mod, website, and backend API.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, credential stuffing, and automated account creation.
  • Respond to support requests and legal obligations.
  • Measure aggregate usage to prioritise engineering work.
  • Show our own promotional banners on the website.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use Minecraft account credentials for any purpose other than authenticating you to Mojang's services on your behalf.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files that a website places in your browser. Local storage is a similar mechanism built into modern browsers. Leaf Client uses both.

4.1 Strictly necessary

We store your session token in browser local storage once you sign in. Without it you cannot remain logged in between page loads. This cannot be disabled through the site while still using authenticated features.

4.2 Analytics

We count usage on our own servers, from requests our services already receive. That covers daily and monthly player counts, playtime totals, how many accounts reach the game after signing up, mod downloads per Minecraft version, how busy each hour of the day is, and how many players are in each country. No third-party analytics product is used, no analytics script runs on the website, and nothing is read from or written to your device for this, so no consent prompt is required. Every figure is a total with no identifier in it.

If we ever introduce a third-party analytics tool, we will name it here and, where required, ask for your consent before it loads.

4.3 Advertising

Leaf Client does not currently use any third-party advertising network. The website may show our own promotional banners, which we serve directly; they set no advertising or tracking cookies and do not profile you. If we add third-party advertising in the future, we will update this policy, name the provider, and, where the law requires it, obtain your consent before any advertising cookie is set.

4.4 Advertising cookies

We do not currently set any advertising cookies, and no third-party ad network runs on the website. If we introduce advertising in the future, visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland will be asked for consent before any advertising cookie is set, and non-personalised ads will be the default without consent.

4.5 Managing cookies in your browser

All major browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will break the login flow.

5. Third-Party Services

Running Leaf Client requires a handful of third-party services. Each of them processes a limited slice of your data under its own privacy policy.

5.1 Advertising

We do not currently use a third-party advertising provider. Any banners shown on the website are our own and are served directly by us, with no third-party ad cookies or profiling. If this changes, we will name the advertising provider here and update the rest of this policy.

5.2 Payment processors

Payments are handled by third-party processors. Card details are collected directly by the processor and never touch our servers. We receive only a transaction identifier, basic billing information, and confirmation of payment. Each processor's handling of your data is governed by its own privacy policy.

5.3 Microsoft OAuth and Mojang services

If you sign in with a Minecraft account we use Microsoft's OAuth flow to authenticate you and Mojang's authentication services to validate your game profile. We receive only the tokens and identifiers needed to confirm that you own a valid Minecraft account. Microsoft and Mojang handle this data under their own policies; we do not store your Microsoft password.

5.4 Discord

Two separate things. Our community server on Discord is an ordinary Discord server. If you join it through the invite link on our site, your use of Discord is governed by Discord's own policies and we receive nothing about you through this Service.

Separately, you can link your Discord account to your Leaf Client account to claim cosmetic rewards. Linking is optional. If you do it, we ask Discord only for the identify scope, which does not include your email address, and we store your Discord user ID, your Discord username, your display name, a link to your avatar image, and the date you linked. You can unlink at any time from your account page, which deletes those fields. Discord's own privacy policy is at discord.com/privacy.

5.5 Third-party services the launcher contacts

When the launcher shows the status of a Minecraft server it asks mcstatus.io about that address. When it draws a player avatar or a skin preview it asks Starlight Skins, Visage, or Crafatar. Your computer makes those requests directly, so the service in question receives the server address or Minecraft username and your IP address. We do not send them anything else, and they are not used to track you across the site.

5.6 Hosting and content delivery

The website, the backend API, and cosmetic asset storage are hosted with commercial cloud providers. These providers process requests on our behalf under data processing agreements but do not use your data for their own purposes.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We share it only in the narrow circumstances below:

  • Service providers. The payment processors, hosting providers, and other third-party services listed in Section 5, to the extent needed to provide their service.
  • Legal requirements. Where compelled by a valid legal process, court order, or applicable law, or where disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Leaf Client, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers. If Leaf Client is ever acquired or merged into another entity, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will give you notice before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.
  • With your consent. Anywhere else, only with your explicit permission.

7. Data Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as there is a legitimate reason to do so:

  • Account records are retained for as long as the account exists. If you delete your account we remove or anonymise your profile within thirty days, except where retention is required for tax, fraud-prevention, or legal reasons.
  • Payment records are retained for the period required by applicable tax law (typically five to seven years).
  • API access logs are retained for up to sixty days for abuse prevention and debugging, then purged or aggregated beyond recognition.
  • Aggregate usage statistics, including the daily per-country player counts described in Section 4.2, are retained for up to twenty-four months. They contain no identifier and are not linked to any account.
  • Support correspondence is retained for up to two years unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of the rights below. We honour these rights worldwide to the extent it is practical to do so.

8.1 For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland (GDPR)

  • Right of access: you can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: you can ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"): you can ask us to delete your account and personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Right to restrict processing, right to object to processing, and right to data portability.
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on your consent.
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Our legal basis for processing is typically the performance of our contract with you (providing the service), our legitimate interest (security, fraud prevention, and counting usage in aggregate), or your consent (advertising cookies, optional analytics).

One limit is worth stating plainly: the aggregate statistics in Section 4.2 hold no identifier, so we cannot find your contribution to them, show it to you, or remove it. Deleting your account does not change those totals, because nothing in them points back to you.

8.2 For users in California (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents may request to know what categories of personal information we collect, the sources and purposes of that collection, and the categories of third parties with whom we share it; to delete personal information; and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. Leaf Client does not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

8.3 Exercising your rights

Email [email protected] with your request. We may ask you to confirm control of the account before acting. We will respond within thirty days (forty-five for complex requests). Using a right will never result in discriminatory treatment.

9. Children's Privacy

Leaf Client is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). If you believe a child has created a Leaf Client account, please contact us and we will remove the account and any associated personal data.

Leaf Client is operated from outside the European Economic Area. Where we transfer personal data out of the EEA, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission and, where necessary, supplementary measures such as encryption in transit. By using the service you acknowledge that your data may be processed in countries whose data protection laws differ from those of your own country.

11. Security

We protect your data with industry-standard measures: TLS on every network hop, hashed passwords, scoped OAuth tokens, hardened server configurations, and restricted internal access. No system is perfectly secure; if we ever discover a breach that affects your personal data we will notify you and the appropriate regulators without undue delay, as required by law.
