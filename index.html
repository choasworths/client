Frequently asked

Read the docs
Is Axquen Client free?
Yes. The launcher, the client and every gameplay feature are free. Cosmetics and Axquen+ are optional and have no effect on gameplay.
Which Minecraft versions does it support?
1.8.9, 1.21 through 1.21.11, and 26.1.2, on Fabric, Forge and NeoForge. You choose the version and loader for each profile in the launcher.
Can I use it on any server?
Axquen has no cheats, only quality-of-life features like the HUD, minimap and zoom. Server owners can switch off features such as Freelook and FullBright over a plugin channel (how it works). Check each server's rules before you play.
How is Axquen Client different from FastClient and other clients?
Axquen Client has its own performance optimizations built into the Axquen mod, and on Fabric it also installs Sodium, Lithium and Entity Culling. Many clients get their FPS gains only from community mods like these. On top of that, Axquen has 35+ built-in mods, voice chat, a HUD Editor, waypoints, a friends list and a smooth, redesigned launcher. In our 26.1.2 Fabric test, Axquen averaged 756 FPS to Lunar Client's 451 (see the comparison). Axquen Client is not affiliated with FastClient or any other client.
