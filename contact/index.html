Response times

We aim to reply to every message within 48 to 72 hours on business days (Monday through Friday). Complex issues, especially those where we have to look at payment logs or talk to a server owner, can take longer. If you haven't heard back within a week, send us a reminder, since something probably got buried.

Discord is usually faster than email for quick questions, but it is not a guaranteed support channel. Formal legal notices should always go to [email protected] so they are tracked.
