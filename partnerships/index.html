Partnerships

Get your server, project or community featured inside Axquen Client.

Axquen Client is a new Minecraft launcher and client, and we work with servers, creators and other projects. Everything on this page is free right now. We'd rather build something people use before we charge anyone for visibility.

What we offer

Featured server slot

Your server appears at the top of the Quick Play section in every Axquen Client launcher, with your icon, tagline, and a one-click join button.

Click analytics

We share aggregate, anonymous click counts so you can see how much traffic the slot sends you. No personal data, just numbers.

Easy updates

Email us to change your icon, tagline, or featured tag at any time. Updates reach every launcher within 30 seconds.

Cross-promotion

We mention featured partners in our Discord announcements and, when it fits, on the Axquen Client blog.

Who we're looking for

  • Active Minecraft servers: Java Edition, any gamemode, any size. We care more about quality than concurrent player counts.
  • Content creators: YouTubers, streamers, and TikTok creators in the Minecraft and client mod space.
  • Tooling and mod projects: texture packs, shader packs, companion mods, server tools. If our players would benefit, we want to hear about it.
  • Communities: Discord servers, subreddits, forums, and tournaments that fit the Axquen Client audience.

Pricing and the future

To be clear: partnerships are completely free right now. Axquen is a new launcher with a small but growing user base, and we'd rather work with partners who joined us early than chase short-term revenue.

Later, once Axquen Client has a much larger active user base, we may introduce paid tiers for premium placement (for example top-of-rail slots, longer feature windows, or more frequent rotation). When that happens:

  • Existing partners get advance notice and grandfathered terms for at least one full feature cycle.
  • A free tier will always exist for community-run, non-commercial projects.
  • We'll publish exact pricing publicly, with no private deals.

For now, all we ask in return is that you keep your server family-friendly, follow Mojang's EULA, and don't harass players. That's it.
